# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful. Critique ideas, evidence, designs, and code rather than attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately humiliate other participants. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

Technical disagreement is expected. Security and engineering claims may be challenged firmly when evidence is weak; that challenge should remain focused on the work.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that materially disrupt the project or violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or project.

If conduct creates a security or privacy concern, follow `SECURITY.md` rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
