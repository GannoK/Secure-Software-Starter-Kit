This file records the licensing boundary for the Secure Software Starter Kit.

## v1.1 and earlier public distribution

The v1.1 public release was offered by Kyle Gannon under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

Rights validly granted to recipients of v1.1 copies remain governed by that historical license and are not retroactively revoked.

The repository state immediately before the proprietary transition is preserved at:

`legacy/v1.1-cc-by-4.0`

Historical boundary commit:

`c6307cb2539ff908928474f4586ed33c2779956a`

## v1.2.0 and later

Beginning with v1.2.0, new and modified material distributed by the copyright owner is offered under the proprietary terms in `LICENSE` unless a specific file or written agreement expressly states otherwise.

This transition does not make historically CC-licensed v1.1 material exclusive again. Anyone with valid rights under the prior CC release may continue exercising those rights according to that license.

## Purpose

The objective of the v1.2.0 transition is to establish a proprietary baseline for future work, maintain a clear chain of title, and ensure contributor/commercial rights are handled through explicit written agreements rather than an automatic public contribution license.
