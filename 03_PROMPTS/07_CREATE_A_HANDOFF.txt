Create a self-contained handoff for a new AI session or human collaborator.

It must be usable without this chat.

Include:
- project/operation name;
- objective;
- governing rules;
- accepted requirements and decisions;
- verified current state;
- documented prior evidence;
- completed work;
- repository/version/branch/commit information when relevant;
- important files;
- services/deployments when relevant;
- tests and security checks;
- unresolved questions;
- blockers;
- known risks;
- prohibited actions;
- work that must not be repeated;
- exact next bounded action;
- exact verification needed to prove that next action succeeds.

Clearly distinguish VERIFIED, USER-PROVIDED, INFERENCE, and UNKNOWN.
