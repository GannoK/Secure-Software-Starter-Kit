# What to say when you are stuck

Copy the prompt that matches your situation.

## I do not know what to do next

> Read the project index and the smallest relevant project files. Tell me the current verified state, the most important unknowns, and the single safest next step. Explain it for a beginner. Do not make changes yet.

## The AI is giving me too much information

> Give me only the next bounded step. Explain where I should perform it, exactly what I should do, what I should expect to see, and what result I should send back before we continue.

## I do not understand a term

> Explain this term in plain English, why it matters to my project, and give one concrete example. Do not assume I know related technical terms.

## Something broke

> Stop proposing fixes. First diagnose. Separate observed evidence from assumptions, identify what changed most recently, and give me the smallest read-only checks that can narrow the cause. Do not reinstall, reset, delete, or broadly reconfigure anything without evidence.

## The chat is getting very long

> Create a self-contained project handoff using the handoff template. Include the mission, accepted decisions, verified state, completed work, current files/versions, tests, unresolved questions, blockers, prohibited actions, and exact next step. Do not rely on this chat being available later.

## I am about to publish

> Perform a release-readiness review against the project requirements, threat model, security baseline, tests, dependency/supply-chain checks, rollback plan, and production release checklist. List blockers separately from recommendations.
