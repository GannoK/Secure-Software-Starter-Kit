1. restate the observed symptom;
2. identify the last known-good state if available;
3. separate evidence from assumptions;
4. list the smallest plausible cause categories;
5. give me the minimum read-only diagnostics that distinguish between them;
6. explain what each diagnostic checks and what the possible outputs mean.

Avoid reinstalling, resetting, deleting, mass-upgrading, disabling security, or changing multiple subsystems until evidence justifies it.
