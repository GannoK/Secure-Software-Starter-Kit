# Project IP Ownership and Chain-of-Title Policy

Status: active internal governance  
Owner of current project-specific copyrightable material: Kyle Gannon, unless a written assignment states otherwise.

## Objective

Maintain a defensible chain of title for software, security guidance, documentation, prompts, templates, examples, diagrams, tooling, and related project assets while clearly separating owned material, historically licensed material, and third-party material.

## Ownership rule

Treat material as project-owned only when there is a documented rights basis, such as:
1. authorship by Kyle Gannon;
2. qualifying employee-created work;
3. legally valid work-made-for-hire treatment with the required writing;
4. signed assignment;
5. documented lawful acquisition.

A Git commit, repository access, invoice, payment, discussion, pull request, attribution, or possession of a file is not by itself sufficient evidence of copyright ownership.

## Historical CC material

v1.1 material was distributed under CC BY 4.0. Preserve that boundary in `../LICENSE_HISTORY.md`. Do not represent the historical grant as revoked or claim exclusivity over rights already granted.

## Contributor intake

Before substantive contributor material is treated as project-owned:
- identify the legal contributor/contracting party;
- determine employee, contractor, vendor, collaborator, or licensor status;
- execute the applicable written IP agreement;
- identify background/pre-existing IP;
- identify third-party dependencies;
- document any promised compensation separately;
- retain executed agreements outside the repository in controlled records.

## Third-party software and standards

Record material third-party dependencies in `../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Review license, redistribution, maintenance, security-support, supply-chain, and commercialization implications before reliance.

## AI-assisted material

Follow `AI_ASSISTED_AUTHORSHIP_POLICY.md`. Preserve meaningful human authorship and provenance where ownership or registration may matter.

## Revenue participation

Ownership and compensation are separate. No royalty, revenue share, equity, or contingent compensation is created by contribution, attribution, or repository participation. Such rights require a separate signed agreement.

## Transfers

Any transfer of project IP to Guild Technology Solutions, another company, buyer, licensee, or holding entity must be documented by a written assignment or transaction instrument.

## Records

Preserve:
- Git history and release boundaries;
- contributor/contractor/employment agreements;
- assignments/licenses;
- third-party license versions;
- SBOM/dependency records where relevant;
- provenance records;
- commercial contracts;
- royalty statements and audit records;
- registration records.

Do not silently rewrite historical authorship or licensing records.
